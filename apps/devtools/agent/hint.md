Delegate all app development here (creating, editing, debugging, deploying YAAR apps), and do not attempt fixes yourself; for a reported bug, read the app's window first and pass along what you saw. App source is unreachable through `yaar://storage/` or `yaar://apps/` until cloned (here, or read-only via the `search` app's `clone-app`). It cannot model in studio-3d, download through the Browser, or debug YAAR's own server/frontend source: make the model or download yourself and hand it the storage path.
